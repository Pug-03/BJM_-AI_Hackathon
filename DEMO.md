# ABLE — 3-minute demo script

**Setup:**
- Open the deployed URL in Chrome, on a laptop and a phone if possible.
- Have the AI and Daily keys set, so the mic, AI and calls work live.
- Make sure no one is signed in.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Landing page. Scroll once past "How it works" and "Speak to fill". | "ABLE is a talent platform for people with disabilities, where disability is never a label, a filter or a price tag." |
| 0:15 | Click **Judge mode** in the header → **Try as talent**. | "Judges get a full sandbox with sample data. No sign-up, and it resets every 24 hours." |
| 0:25 | Talent feed → open **your profile** (avatar menu on desktop, **Profile** tab on a phone). Show the tabs and the "Finish your profile" pill; for reviews, open a sample profile from **Explore** (e.g. Kittipong) → Reviews tab. | "A freelancer-style profile: real work, certificates, reviews. Date of birth, gender and the 'work setup I need' note stay private." |
| 0:50 | Open **Edit profile** (`/onboarding/talent`). Click **Fill my profile by voice** → speak for 15 seconds in Thai or English → review the draft fields → **Add**. | "Speak to fill: you talk, Claude splits it into fields, and you check each one. Every AI suggestion is labelled until you keep it." |
| 1:20 | On step 2, tap the mic on **Headline**, say a job title, then try **Write with AI** on the bio. | "Every text field has a mic. The audio is deleted right after it's transcribed." |
| 1:35 | Judge banner → **Switch to employer**. Show the feed with matched skills (violet chips with a check) and **Why this match**. | "Matching combines embeddings, skill overlap, work fit and completeness. The reason is written by AI from the matched facts only." |
| 2:00 | **Star** a card. Open **Filters** and point out the note. | "There's no disability, gender, religion, ethnicity or age filter anywhere. The schema rejects them." |
| 2:15 | **Message** on a card → send a message → Add (+) → **Send offer** (role, pay). | "Pay is agreed between the two people. ABLE never suggests a rate. An offer is a message card, not a contract." |
| 2:35 | Click the **video call** button → Daily opens → **Leave call**. Show the call card in the thread. | "Voice and video calls are built in; the other side gets a Join card." |
| 2:50 | Footer → **Roadmap** (built vs planned). Close on the landing CTA. | "Everything you saw works today. Identity verification is next. ABLE: work that fits you." |

**Backup if a key is missing:** voice-fill hides "Type instead" without keys (it needs Claude either way). Every AI or call button opens a short "not set up here" explanation instead of failing. Narrate the flow from there.
